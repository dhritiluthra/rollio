Backend ✅
  └── POST /auth/register
  └── POST /auth/login
  └── POST /carts              (create cart)
  └── PUT  /carts/location     (set location)
  └── PUT  /carts/toggle       (go live / go offline)
  └── GET  /carts/nearby       (find active carts)
